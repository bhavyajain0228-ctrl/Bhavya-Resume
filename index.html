About Me

“I enjoy being the person who turns a big, messy idea into something that actually happens on time.”

I'm Bhavya, a first-year student at PGDAV College, University of Delhi, where I'm studying Commerce and Economics. Alongside that, I've been admitted to IIM Bangalore's online BBA in Digital Business & Entrepreneurship, which I'm genuinely excited about.

Most of my school years went into Model United Nations, editorial work and public speaking. I've helped run a magazine from scratch, led my school's MUN society and worked on the Secretariat of conferences, which taught me more about teamwork, deadlines and staying calm under pressure than any classroom could.

Right now I'm trying to understand where economics, business and public policy meet, and how decisions made in one world ripple through the others. I'm always happy to talk about current affairs, so feel free to say hello.
